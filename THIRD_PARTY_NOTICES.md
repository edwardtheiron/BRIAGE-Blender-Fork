### Modifications

The `BRIAGE/` directory contains the independently maintained fork.

The fork currently contains modifications for Blender 4.2 compatibility,
IGS import restoration, bug fixes, and usability improvements.

The modified version is explicitly identified as a fork and is
distributed separately from the original releases.
